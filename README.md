# ☕ BrewBerry Café Sales Analysis Dashboard

> An end-to-end Data Analytics project focused on analyzing coffee shop sales data to uncover actionable business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**BrewBerry Café Pvt. Ltd.** is a fictional premium coffee shop chain operating multiple outlets across metropolitan cities. Despite increasing customer footfall, management observed inconsistent sales performance, inefficient inventory planning, and varying store profitability.

As a **Data Analyst**, I analyzed transactional sales data and developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to identify customer purchasing patterns, peak business hours, top-performing products, and store-level performance, enabling management to make informed business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The management lacked visibility into:

- Store-wise sales performance
- Peak sales hours
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Best-selling products
- Monthly revenue trends
- Product category performance
- Average customer spending

Without these insights, the business faced:

- Overstocking of slow-moving products
- Stock shortages of popular items
- Inefficient staff scheduling
- Missed revenue opportunities
- Reduced operational efficiency

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The objective of this project is to analyze coffee shop sales data and answer the following business questions:

- How do sales vary by day of the week and hour of the day?
- What are the peak business hours?
- What is the monthly sales revenue?
- Which store locations perform the best?
- What is the average bill value per customer?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which product categories contribute the most to sales?

# 📈 Key Business Insights

### ⏰ Peak Business Hours

- Customer demand peaks between **7 AM and 10 AM**
- Highest sales occur around **9 AM**
- Sales decline significantly after evening hours

**Recommendation:** Increase staffing and inventory during morning rush hours.

---

### ☕

Coffee Drives Revenue

- Coffee contributes the highest share of total sales.
- Bakery products are the second-largest revenue contributor.

**Recommendation:** Introduce Coffee + Bakery combo offers.

---

### 🏪 Store Performance

- Hell's Kitchen recorded the highest revenue.
- Astoria maintained similar customer footfall.
- Lower Manhattan generated comparatively lower sales.

**Recommendation:** Replicate successful strategies across all locations.

---

### ⭐ Best-Selling Products

Top revenue-generating products include:

- Barista Espresso
- Brewed Chai Tea
- Gourmet Brewed Coffee
- Hot Chocolate
- Brewed Black Tea

**Recommendation:** Prioritize inventory and promotions for these products.

---

### 📅 Weekly Sales Trend

- Sales remain relatively consistent throughout the week.
- No major difference between weekdays and weekends.

**Recommendation:** Launch weekday promotional campaigns to increase revenue.

---

### 🛒 Customer Buying Behavior

- Average Orders per Customer: **1.44**
- Average Bill per Customer: **₹4.69**

**Recommendation:** Increase basket size using upselling and combo deals.
